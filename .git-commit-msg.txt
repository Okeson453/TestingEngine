Root-cause investigation of the 19:05-19:12 window (uniform 570-1001ms
slow queries in BOTH pools — critical SELECT 1 = 595ms, general
worker_state / live_round_state / worker_locks / outbox dead-letter =
570-624ms, critical outbox claim = 1001ms — with ZERO pool_acquire_ms
warnings all window; BG reconcile 927-1420ms in some rounds and a
1.0-1.2s skip-to-not-persisted gap in others, never both):

FINDING 1 — NOT pool contention. Zero acquire warns (100ms threshold)
while queries ran 3-6x slow. Pool acquire is exonerated by the logs.

FINDING 2 — NOT lock contention or plan pathology. SELECT 1 has no
locks, no scans, no contention; it took 595ms on the critical pool.
The outbox claim (WITH picked ... FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED) is exactly
matched by migration 0041's partial index. The BG reconcile CTE is a
single round trip on disjoint tables (pass 8 audit).

FINDING 3 — the BG slow phase MOVES between rounds: reconcile TX slow
in some rounds (19:07:28 = 883ms, 19:08:22 = 1420ms, 19:11:48 = 927ms),
the memory-only skip-to-not-persisted gap slow in others (19:07:01 =
1222ms, 19:07:44 = 1034ms, 19:08:51 = 1118ms) — every operation in that
gap is synchronous (completeTarget, recordAttempt, logger.info). A
delay in a no-DB phase cannot be a database wait. Total BG-event-to-
NO_BET cost is ~1.0-1.4s in slow rounds and ~0ms in fast ones: one
~1.2s stall per affected round, landing wherever the event loop happens
to be when it hits.

DIAGNOSIS: two candidates remain — (a) event-loop stall on the Railway
instance, (b) Neon network/compute RTT spike. queryMs (client.query
wall time) inflates under BOTH without touching pool acquires, and a
loop stall also explains the no-DB phase delay. The 19:05-19:12 log
cannot separate them. Shipped instrumentation that will:
